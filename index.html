Stay safe, whichever platform you pick

  • A real client never asks you to pay to get a job, a "registration fee", or a deposit.
  • Keep chats, payments and contracts inside the platform. Be careful when someone asks you to move to WhatsApp or Telegram before any work is agreed.
  • Fake "like and review tasks for money" offers are a common scam. Real platforms do not work that way.
  • Read the fee and withdrawal terms before you start. Fees and rules change often.
  • Start small, deliver well, and ask for a review. Reviews are your real currency.
